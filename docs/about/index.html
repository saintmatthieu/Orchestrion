Orchestrion

What is Orchestrion ?

Consider music in three dimensions: time, loudness and pitch.

When you play an instrument, you normally control all three dimensions. Getting all three of them right means playing the rhythm correctly, with the correct dynamics, and of course, hitting the right notes, all at once. This requires practice – a little practice for beginner pieces (Frère Jacques), lifetime practice for virtuosic ones (Chopin’s Fantaisie Impromptu). It’s a pity: either you’re talented enough and you spend thousands of hours practicing, or you’re reduced to listening.

… Are you? Not with Orchestrion! Orchestrion implements ways of playing just the rhythm of a score, and it will play the rest. Now you do more than listen passively: you engage with the music, you discover it from the inside. And it develops your skills: playing just the rhythm is almost trivial for some scores, but for others, it might be just the right challenge for you. Or for your kids, to get them started with music. Mine love it.

How to play Orchestrion

You can play it with your computer keyboard. Then you’re in control of rhythm, Orchestrion reads the dynamics and notes from the score. If you have a MIDI keyboard, you can also take control of the dynamics (and find out how important they are to the quality of an interpretation!) or you can decide to control the notes, too.

You can choose to play both hands, or just one of them and have the other hand automatically follow, too!

Whatever play mode you choose, you can make it sound great. Orchestrion is not just a practice app, it is meant to sound good! There’s no pianist in your band and you want to play Guns N’ Roses’ November Rain? Consider Orchestrion! I have a PhD in digital signal processing, am a developer on the Audacity team, and can tell you that I care about sound quality.
